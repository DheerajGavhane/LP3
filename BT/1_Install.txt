Blockchain is a decentralized digital network that stores transactional records in blocks. These blocks are connected to one another using cryptographic techniques.
meaning that no single authority controls the entire network. Copies of the ledger are maintained by multiple participants in the network.

Features of Blockchain
1. Decentralization
- No single person or organization controls the entire blockchain.
2. Immutability
- Once information is confirmed and recorded, it is extremely difficult to modify.
3. Tamper resistance
- Blockchain uses cryptographic hashes and linked blocks.
- This makes unauthorized modifications detectable.
4. Transparency
- Public blockchain transactions can be viewed by network participants.
5. Security
- Cryptography and consensus mechanisms help protect transactions and records

Applications of Blockchain
Application	Use
Banking	Secure digital payments and transfers
Healthcare	Maintaining medical records
Cryptocurrency	Digital currency transactions

MetaMask is a digital wallet that allows user to store cryptocurrency that allows users to manage blockchain accounts and interact with decentralized applications (dApps)
Ether is the native cryptocurrency of the Ethereum blockchain.
It is used for:
- Sending and receiving digital currency.
- Paying transaction fees.
- Interacting with smart contracts.
- Using decentralized applications.

A gas fee is the transaction fee paid to the Ethereum network for processing a transaction.
1. Public Blockchain
- Open to everyone.
- Anyone can participate according to the network rules.
- Transactions can be publicly verified.
- Examples: Bitcoin and Ethereum.
2. Private Blockchain
- Controlled by a single organization.
- Only authorized users can participate.
- Used for internal business operations.
3. Consortium Blockchain
- Controlled by a group of organizations.
- Multiple organizations share control.
- Used in banking and supply chain management.
4. Hybrid Blockchain
- Combination of public and private blockchain features.
- Some information is public, while other information is restricted.
- Used in enterprise applications.
